# Purchase Calculations

- Product gross = Quantity × Purchase Cost.
- Product discount amount = Product gross × Purchase Discount % / 100.
- Product net = Product gross − Product discount amount.
- Bill discount amount = Sum of product net values × Bill Discount % / 100.
- Expenses amount = (Sum of product net values − Bill discount amount) × Expenses % / 100.
- Total Amount = Product net subtotal − Bill Discount + Expenses.
- Balance = Total Amount − Paid Amount.

Trade Offer is stored separately from monetary discounts. A trade offer may represent bonus/free quantity (e.g. 10+1) and must not automatically be treated as a cash discount. If the shop configures Trade Offer as a percentage benefit, the calculation rule must be explicit before affecting stock/cost.
